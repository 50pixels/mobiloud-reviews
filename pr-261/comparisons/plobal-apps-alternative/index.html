Plobal Apps vs MobiLoud: FAQs

Have any more questions we didn’t answer? Book a call or send us a message to clear it all up.

Is Plobal Apps only for Shopify?

Plobal Apps works with Shopify stores only. If your store is on Magento, BigCommerce, WooCommerce or another platform, Plobal isn’t an option. MobiLoud works with all of them, Shopify included.

Does Plobal take a share of my app sales?

Plobal takes a share of your app sales on two plans: the free Starter plan takes 3% of in-app sales, and Enterprise takes 1% on top of its monthly price. MobiLoud doesn’t take a share of your app sales.

How many integrations can I use with Plobal?

The number of integrations you can use with Plobal depends on the plan: 2 on Starter, 4 on Growth, 7 on Essential and unlimited on Enterprise, from a list of 41. With MobiLoud, there’s no list: every app on your store works in the app.

Which is cheaper, Plobal or MobiLoud?

Plobal is cheaper on the monthly price. It starts free with a share of sales, and its paid plans range from $199 to $1,199 a month. MobiLoud starts at $799 a month with no share of your sales, which covers the design, the build, the launch and a team that keeps your app updated.

Which is faster to launch?

Plobal is faster to launch, at under a week in its case studies. MobiLoud apps usually go live in 2 to 4 weeks, including the store reviews, because our team designs, builds and tests the app for you.

Is a MobiLoud app a native app?

A MobiLoud app is a native iOS and Android app, built in Swift and Kotlin. It uses a mix of your existing web code and custom elements built for the app, such as native navigation, push notifications and login, and customers get an app that looks and feels like any other on their phone.

Can I move my Plobal app to MobiLoud without losing my users?

You can move your Plobal app to MobiLoud without losing your users, as long as the app is in your own Apple and Google developer accounts. We publish the new app as an update to that listing, so your users get a normal update and your ratings and reviews stay, and we time the switch so the new app is live before your Plobal plan ends.

Do I have to build and maintain the app myself with MobiLoud?

You don’t build or maintain the app yourself with MobiLoud. We design and build it, publish it to both stores and update it for every new iOS and Android release, while your team keeps working on your store.
