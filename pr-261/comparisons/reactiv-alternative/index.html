Reactiv vs MobiLoud: Which Shopify App Builder Is Right For Your Store?

Compare how Reactiv and MobiLoud build apps for Shopify brands. See the differences in custom design, website functions, app management and pricing.

Last reviewed: November 1, 2026

Reactiv vs MobiLoud in 30 Seconds

Reactiv is the better fit if you

  • Want App Clips, so iPhone shoppers can buy without downloading
  • Want a mobile app built and managed separately from your site
  • Want a separate workspace to build and update your app

MobiLoud is the better fit if you

  • Want to carry over every app and feature from your site
  • Want the app to look and feel like your site
  • Want to manage app and site from the same place
  • Are on a custom Shopify build (or not on Shopify)

How they work

Building with Reactiv vs MobiLoud

With app builders like Reactiv, you’re managing two separate storefronts: your website and your app. MobiLoud keeps it all unified: one storefront, two unique channels.

Integrations

Your integrations come with you

Reactiv comes with integrations for about 60 well-known Shopify apps. MobiLoud integrates with all of them. Every app, integration, custom feature and theme customization carries over to your mobile app, by default.

Reactiv

Each tool, with its own integration

A tool that isn’t on Reactiv’s list needs a custom integration, just to carry over what already works on your site.

MobiLoud

Every integration, carried over with your site

If an integration works on your site, it works in the app. No separate integration adding more complexity to your app.

Website improvements

Improve your website and app together

Reactiv provides a separate workspace for building and updating your app. With MobiLoud, changes you make on your website appear in the app too.

Reactiv

Create and update app screens

Reactiv builds and edits screens from a prompt, an image or your voice. Its scheduler refreshes your layouts on a schedule, with your approval.

MobiLoud

Your website improvements reach your app

Your team keeps improving the website it already manages. Those changes reach your app automatically, without another editor or publishing workflow.

Reactiv’s app-specific features give your team another place to make changes.

With MobiLoud, your team keeps one website to manage.

Your search, recommendations and personalized content work on both channels.

Reactiv vs MobiLoud: FAQs

Have any more questions we didn’t answer? Book a call or send us a message to clear it all up.

Book a call
Is Reactiv only for Shopify?

Yes. Reactiv is built exclusively for Shopify. If your store is on Magento, BigCommerce, WooCommerce or another platform, Reactiv isn’t an option. MobiLoud works with all of them, Shopify included.

Can MobiLoud build features just for the app?

Yes. You can easily create app-only pages and designs through your web platform; we’ll help you tag these pages so they exclusively show up in your app. We can also build custom native features for your app, such as Face ID integrations, barcode scanners and custom SDK integrations. Book a free demo and let us know what you need, and we’ll create a custom proposal for you.

Which is cheaper, Reactiv or MobiLoud?

MobiLoud starts lower. Our price starts at $799 a month, and Reactiv’s Scaling Brands plan starts at $1,500 a month. Both offer custom-scoped pricing for stores with more extensive needs.

Which is faster to launch?

Reactiv quotes a 2-4 week launch time, we quote 2-4 weeks. The difference is negligible; Reactiv’s quoted number is slightly faster.

Will my Shopify apps work in a MobiLoud app?

Yes. The apps you use for reviews, loyalty, subscriptions, search, bundles and everything else on your store work in the app the way they do on your site. No separate integrations needed.

Is a Reactiv app more native than a MobiLoud app?

Reactiv builds every screen in React Native, separately from your website. A MobiLoud app is built on your website, with native tabs, navigation and push added on top, so it looks and works like your site. Whichever one is “more native” is beside the point. Your customers don’t care: they care that it’s a real mobile app that works, and both options do that for you.

Can I move my Reactiv app to MobiLoud without losing my users?

Yes. As long as the app is in your own Apple and Google developer accounts, we can publish the new app as an update to that listing. Your users get a normal update, and your ratings and reviews stay. We time the switch to make sure there’s no downtime for your users.
