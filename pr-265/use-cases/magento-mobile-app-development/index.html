How it works

What MobiLoud does for your Magento store

MobiLoud turns your existing Magento storefront into full featured native apps for iOS and Android. It's powered by your live storefront, so every extension, every custom module and every enhancement your agency built works in your app as it does on the web.

Designed for the phone, built on your website

MobiLoud designs your app around your brand, keeping your checkout and website features working. Update your site once and the app updates with it.

  1. 1

    The app is built on your live storefront

    No templates, and nothing rebuilt against GraphQL. The app loads the storefront you already run, so your theme, your extensions and your checkout come with it.

  2. 2

    Your app, designed for the phone

    MobiLoud's team adds the native pieces: tab bar and navigation, push notifications, deep linking, social sign-in and custom screens, then ships your app as iOS and Android binaries.

  3. 3

    We ship it under your accounts

    Our team handles store review and submission, and the app goes live under your own Apple and Google accounts. You keep merchandising from the Magento admin exactly as you do now.

Your storefront, as a real native app

You get an app with all the features customers expect from a native app: tab bar, navigation, gestures, push and login, all native, all built into the app. We've had 2,000+ apps published on the App Store and Google Play, and know what your app needs to look and perform at its best.

Native tab bar and navigation Push notifications via OneSignal, or Klaviyo on Business and Enterprise Deep links into any URL App-only pages and experiences Persistent login Google, Apple and Facebook sign-in Smart app banners App analytics Ad campaign attribution

What carries over

Everything you've built, working in your app

Most app builders can't replicate what a Magento storefront does. They rebuild your catalogue against the API, and the extensions, custom modules and pricing logic you have spent years on are left behind.

MobiLoud works the other way. Our team builds your app on the website you already have. MobiLoud keeps everything from your storefront in the app, including the customization that makes it yours.

Your theme, whatever it is

Luma, a heavily customized child theme or a Hyvä front end, the app renders whatever you serve. Years of agency work carry over unchanged.

Every extension and custom module

Marketplace extensions and the bespoke modules written for your store keep working, because they keep running on your storefront rather than being re-integrated.

Your checkout

Customers check out through your existing checkout, with your payment methods, tax rules, shipping logic and any custom checkout steps intact.

Multi-website and store views

Multiple websites, store views, currencies and languages work in one app, and each can get its own menu, push notifications and onboarding.

Customer groups and pricing

Customer groups, tier pricing, catalog price rules and cart price rules apply in the app exactly as configured, so a logged-in buyer sees their own prices.

Wholesale and trade accounts

Whether B2B behaviour comes from extensions or from code your agency wrote, it behaves in the app the way it behaves on the web.

Search and merchandising

Elasticsearch, Algolia, category rules and CMS blocks are live in the app the moment they are live on the web.

Your ERP and your hosting

Nothing moves. The app points at the storefront you already host, so there is no migration, no sync job and no second source of truth.

Will my Magento extensions work in the mobile app?

Yes. A MobiLoud app loads your live storefront, so every Marketplace extension and every custom module written for your store keeps working, because it keeps running on your storefront. There is nothing to re-integrate, no compatibility list to check, and no module that has to be ported to a second stack.

Does my Magento theme carry over to the app?

Yes. Whether you run Luma, a heavily customized child theme or a Hyvä front end, the app renders exactly what your storefront renders. Years of agency front-end work come through as built, and a template change you deploy today is in the app today.

Do I need to go headless or move to PWA Studio first?

No. MobiLoud works with whatever front end you serve today, so there is no replatforming prerequisite and no PWA build to commission. If you migrate your front end later, the app points at the new one rather than being rebuilt, and you keep your listings, ratings and install base.

Does my Magento checkout change?

No. Customers check out through your existing checkout, with your payment methods, tax configuration, shipping rules and any custom checkout steps intact. No payment routing moves to a third party, and your order flow is unchanged.

Do customer groups and tier pricing work in the app?

Yes. Customer groups, tier pricing, catalog price rules and cart price rules apply in the app exactly as they are configured, because the app renders the same storefront that already applies them. A logged-in wholesale buyer sees their own prices without any extra setup.

Can one app serve multiple websites or store views?

Yes. A multi-website or multi-store-view setup can run as a single app that switches between them, or as separate apps, whichever fits how you sell. Each can have its own navigation, push notification audience and onboarding, and multi-currency and multi-language work exactly as they do on the web.

How long does it take to build a Magento mobile app?

Most Magento storefronts go live on the App Store and Google Play in 2 to 4 weeks from kickoff. That covers discovery and design in week one, build and testing in weeks one and two, followed by app store submission and review.

How much does a Magento mobile app cost?

MobiLoud is a flat monthly subscription that covers the build, the launch and ongoing maintenance, with no revenue share and no per-transaction fee. Building the same app natively runs to $250K to $1M+ a year once you count the developers, QA and store maintenance it takes to keep two codebases alive.

What does my agency have to do after launch?

Keep shipping to the storefront the way they do now. There is no app QA gate on a Magento release, no second codebase and no GraphQL schema to extend. We handle the native side permanently: OS releases, SDK deprecations, certificate renewals and store policy changes.
