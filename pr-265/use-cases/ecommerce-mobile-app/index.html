  • Custom features and integrations wait on their roadmap

  • Some charge 1.75% to 2.5% of in-app revenue

MobiLoud

  • Built on the store you already run, not a copy of it

  • One store, one release cycle, one team

  • Live on both stores in 2 to 4 weeks

The process

Go live in 2 to 4 weeks

We help you bring your store to life as native mobile apps, in weeks, not months.

  1. Days 1 to 7

    Free preview

    Before anything is signed, we build a working iOS and Android preview from your live store. You evaluate your own app, not a deck.

  2. Week 1

    Kickoff

    Onboarding call to agree configuration and the launch plan, plus access to your store and developer accounts. Security review runs in parallel if you need one.

  3. Weeks 2 to 4

    Build, integrate, test

    Native navigation and design tuned to your brand, push wired into your OneSignal account (or Klaviyo on Business and Enterprise), analytics into GA4 and Firebase. QA on real iOS and Android devices, then your sign-off.

  4. Weeks 2 to 4

    Launch

    Submitted to the App Store and Google Play under your accounts. We manage both reviews, typically one to two weeks. Then the launch playbook goes live: smart banners, install prompts, an announcement to your list.

  5. After launch

    Growth support & maintenance

    Our team handles all the app-specific maintenance needed after you go live, along with providing support and guidance to help you get the most out of your app channel.

A custom native app typically costs $150,000 to $1M+ to build, and it keeps costing money every year for the team, QA and store upkeep. MobiLoud starts at $799 a month, which covers the build, the launch and the team that keeps the app updated.

With MobiLoud, it takes roughly 2 to 4 weeks from kickoff to both apps being live on the App Store and Google Play, including review and submission. A custom native build typically takes 6 to 12 months to reach version one.

MobiLoud works for any ecommerce platform: Shopify and Shopify Plus, BigCommerce, Adobe Commerce and Magento, WooCommerce, Salesforce Commerce Cloud, VTEX, Shopware, PrestaShop, headless builds and fully custom sites. All work the same way, because it’s your live site powering the app, not a platform’s API.

No. This is the main difference between MobiLoud and custom native development. A native build needs every product, price, promotion, cart rule and account behavior exposed through an API and then re-implemented on the mobile side. MobiLoud uses your live, existing storefront, so there are no APIs needed.

With MobiLoud, you get both iOS and Android binaries, but they’re always in sync. Because they both pull from your live website, you’re not juggling two separate platforms or making the same updates twice.

If your store is fast, your app will be too. Modern technology means there’s minimal difference in performance between an app powered by your website, and a fully custom app built from scratch. The better your store performs on the web, the better it will perform in your app, and MobiLoud’s approach to building it doesn’t add any meaningful lag or latency.

Push notifications get sent through a push notification provider, similar to an email marketing service. The app sends them through the device’s native delivery mechanism, which means there’s no carrier, algorithm or inbox filter to contend with. In MobiLoud apps, you can use OneSignal on every plan, or Klaviyo on Business and Enterprise, to send native push notifications, with all the segmentation, scheduling and other tools you need to build and manage push automations.

Yes. Apps powered by your website can be published to the app stores. The basic requirement is that they provide native capabilities beyond what your website does, and MobiLoud’s native layer does that. MobiLoud has successfully published over 2,000 apps since 2013, and handles the full submission and review process for you.

Yes. Switching to MobiLoud is a smooth process. The MobiLoud team builds new apps based on your current storefront, and ships them as an update to your existing app listing, which means your users don’t need to download a new app; they automatically receive the new version as soon as they update.

The apps are submitted under your own Apple Developer and Google Play Console accounts, so the listings, reviews, install base, analytics and customer relationships are all yours. MobiLoud retains IP in the platform and the code that builds the app. If you ever move to another solution, the listings, reviews and users stay with you.
