Pricing & Billing

Do I keep my app if I cancel? +

Your developer accounts, listings, ratings and install base remain yours. When the subscription ends, the MobiLoud app is removed or replaced with another vendor's app. A custom app you build later ships as an update to the same listing, so your existing users come with it.

What happens as app usage grows? +

Your app keeps running as normal. Your price is based on monthly active users and changes as the app grows. We review usage quarterly against the previous three months, so one busy month does not set your bill on its own.

What payment methods do you accept? +

MobiLoud accepts credit card payments. Invoicing, bank transfer and purchase orders are available on agreed terms.

What are the payment terms? +

You pay the monthly or annual subscription and any add-ons you choose, due at sign-up for the first period. You can ask for a free app preview before signing up.

Is your pricing always subscription-based, or do you offer a one-time fee? +

We always use a subscription model because work on a mobile app doesn’t end once it’s live. Apps require ongoing maintenance to keep up with SDK updates for iOS and Android version changes, as well as updates for the App Store and Google Play requirements.

Additionally, key components like push notification and analytics SDKs, along with our platform, need regular updates for security and performance.

A subscription also ensures you have access to support whenever you need it-even years after launch, whether for something as small as updating app store screenshots or more significant adjustments. This model provides the reliability and ongoing service you can count on.

How does MobiLoud calculate fees based on active user numbers? +

Fees are based on monthly active users, measured as unique devices that opened the app at least once in a 30-day window, reported in Google Analytics or Firebase.

MobiLoud starts at $799 per month. See pricing

Usage is reviewed quarterly against the previous three months, so a single busy month does not set your bill on its own.

Do I need to sign a contract with MobiLoud? +

You subscribe on our standard terms, with no negotiated contract to work through before you can start.

Custom contracts and payment terms are available on agreed terms. We start from our own agreement, and your legal team is welcome to review it and send redlines.

Do we need to register for Apple and Google Developer accounts? +

Yes, you need to register as a business for both Apple and Google developer accounts. This is so you're fully in control of your mobile app and you own the relationship with both Google and Apple.

To register, your business will need a D-U-N-S Number. This is a unique nine-digit identifier assigned to businesses by Dun & Bradstreet, used worldwide to verify a company’s legal status and identity. Both Apple and Google use this number to ensure that your business is legitimate.

Once you have the D-U-N-S Number, you can proceed with registering for both the Apple Developer Program, which costs $99 USD per year, and the Google Play Developer Program, which has a one-time registration fee of $25.

We encourage you to initiate the registration process with Apple and Google right after signing up with us, to avoid any hold-ups later. By the time we have completed work on your app, your accounts should be ready to go.

Note we don't support individual or personal accounts.

What are active users? Why do I pay more when my app gets more users? +

Active users are unique devices that open your app in a 30-day window. Downloads alone do not count, and two devices count as two. You can track this easily using services like Google Firebase or Google Analytics.

We charge based on active users for two key reasons. First, it ensures our business model is aligned with your success. As your app grows, our pricing scales with it, meaning we're invested in helping you increase engagement and revenue. We provide ongoing support to help you make the most of features like push notifications and work with you on strategies to grow your app’s impact.

Second, more active users create higher demands on our systems and support. As your app becomes more popular, it requires additional resources, technical assistance, and system capacity. By charging proportionally, we can ensure that we’re able to maintain a high level of service and support as your app scales, even if you reach millions of users.

How can I offer app-exclusive products and discounts in my Shopify app? +

MobiLoud lets you create a unique shopping experience for your app users with app-exclusive products and discounts for Shopify stores. This helps drive more engagement and purchases through your mobile app.

App-Exclusive Products

You can mark specific products as only available in the app, ensuring they do not appear on your website. This is done through Shopify’s metafields, and we provide a simple setup process to make it work. Read more on App Exclusive Products.

Once configured:

  • Products marked as app-exclusive will only be visible and purchasable inside the app.
  • These products will be hidden from the online store, but users on the app will see them normally.
  • You can promote these products through push notifications to drive conversions.

App-Exclusive Discounts

You can also offer discount codes that only work within the app, encouraging more users to shop through the app instead of the website. Read more on App Exclusive Discounts.

Once enabled:

  • You choose the app-only coupons in the MobiLoud app under Settings > Discounts: every code starting with a text you choose (such as "APP"), specific coupons, or both.
  • If a shopper enters an app-only code on the website, it is removed at checkout with a message asking them to use the app. In the app, it applies normally, including Buy it now and express checkout.
  • This feature is available on every Shopify plan except Starter.

If you need help setting up app-exclusive products or discounts, our team is available to assist.

Are there any limitations to MobiLoud? +

The limitations of MobiLoud are primarily the same as those of your website. If it works in a browser, we can convert it into a mobile app.

There are some limitations for accessing certain native device features, such as the accelerometer, Face ID, or complex hardware integrations like Bluetooth and NFC. These are all out of scope for the type of applications that we provide.

If you require deep native functionality, MobiLoud might not be the best fit, as we focus on leveraging your existing web app or website to create an app and native integrations would be too complex.

For fully custom native features or hybrid apps built from scratch, solutions like Cordova or React Native might be better suited.

Our solution is ideal for most web-based apps or websites that don’t rely on highly specialized native functions.

MobiLoud is not ideal for apps requiring deep native features, such as:

- Banking apps needing Face ID or NFC payments.
- Fitness or health apps using sensors or wearables.
- Bluetooth-connected apps for IoT or smart devices.
- Games needing advanced graphics or real-time rendering.
- Video conferencing apps needing real-time multimedia control.
- Apps relying on NFC for payments or access.

For these cases, custom native or hybrid app development is a better fit.

You also have access to a dashboard where you can control app-specific features (like push notifications or app settings) without needing technical knowledge. If you're not comfortable managing these aspects, our team is always available to handle it for you.

Overall, the app requires minimal effort to keep running, allowing you to focus on your website while enjoying the benefits of both a fully functional mobile app, always up to date.

Can I automate push notifications for new content? +

Yes, you can configure the notifications to go out automatically as a response to specific events, including new content getting published. You can also send notifications manually whenever you wish.

Our WordPress plugins and Shopify apps provide this functionality for you, so there's nothing for your team to build.

If your site doesn't run on WordPress or Shopify, but rather uses any popular web stack, you'll be able to easily connect to Onesignal's REST API to trigger notifications for any event.

Will I have to update a dashboard whenever there’s new content? +

No. The apps will update automatically whenever you publish anything new! There is little to add to your existing workflow, just keep doing what you're doing, easy like that!
