Why push

Stop paying to reach your own customers

Inboxes are crowded, SMS costs more with every message, and retargeting ads charge you to reach people who already know your brand. Push notifications go straight to the lock screens of your best customers, with no per-send cost.

  • No cost per message

    Each message costs you nothing extra to send, making push the most cost-effective channel you have.

  • Instant reach

    Notifications arrive moments after you send them, ideal for flash sales, product drops and abandoned cart reminders.

  • Right on the lock screen

    Push shows up on the lock screen with your app icon, not buried in an inbox or a promotions tab.

  • An audience you own

    Your push subscribers are yours to reach whenever you like, with no ad auction, no algorithm and no feed deciding who sees your message.

  • Your most engaged customers

    The people who install your app and allow notifications are your most engaged customers, and most likely to take action when they see your messages.

  • Minutes to write

    A push is a title and a line of text. There’s no design or template to build, so a campaign goes from idea to lock screen in minutes.

Push notifications: FAQs

How do push notifications work in a MobiLoud app?

Push notifications in a MobiLoud app are native iOS and Android notifications, sent through your own OneSignal account, or Klaviyo on Business and Enterprise. Our team builds push into your app and connects your account, and your team sends campaigns and automated messages from that platform. Each notification opens the page you link to, inside the app.

Which push notification platforms does MobiLoud work with?

MobiLoud apps send push notifications through OneSignal or, on Business and Enterprise, Klaviyo, two of the leading platforms for push and customer messaging. On Enterprise, we also connect Attentive, Salesforce Marketing Cloud and other push providers.

Do I need my own OneSignal or Klaviyo account?

You send push through your own OneSignal account (or Klaviyo on Business and Enterprise), which our team connects to your app during setup. With OneSignal, your team sends and schedules from the OneSignal dashboard, and OneSignal bills you directly: its free plan covers 1,000 monthly active app users and is paid above that. With Klaviyo, push sits in the same account as your email and SMS.

Can I send push notifications from Klaviyo?

You can send push notifications from Klaviyo once your app is connected to your Klaviyo account. Push then works in your campaigns and flows, with the same profiles and segments you use for email and SMS, and app users are merged with the profiles Klaviyo already has.

Can I send abandoned cart push notifications?

Abandoned cart push notifications work in two ways in a MobiLoud app. The app can schedule its own reminder when a shopper leaves items in their cart, with the message and delay you choose, and cancel it once the cart is empty. If you use Klaviyo, you can also add push to your abandoned cart flow.

Can customers get order and shipping updates by push?

Shopify stores can send order and shipping updates by push through the MobiLoud app in Shopify admin. The messages can include details like the order number, total and shipping method, and they reach customers who are signed in to your app.

When are customers asked to allow notifications?

Your app can ask customers to allow notifications the first time they open it, or at a later moment you choose. Many brands ask from a welcome screen that explains what customers will get, or from a button on their site, because a request with context gets more people to say yes.

Can I send push notifications to specific customers?

You can send push notifications to a segment or to a single customer. Your site can tag app users by interest in OneSignal or set profile attributes in Klaviyo, and linking app users to their accounts lets you message one person, for example when their order is delivered.

How do I measure the results of push notifications?

You measure push results in OneSignal (or Klaviyo on Business and Enterprise), which report how many notifications were delivered and opened, and in Google Analytics, where UTM tags on each notification show the visits and sales it brought in.

How are app push notifications different from web push?

App push notifications come from your own app, with your icon on the lock screen, and open straight into the app. Web push notifications come from a browser, and on iPhone they only work for sites that a customer has added to their Home Screen.
