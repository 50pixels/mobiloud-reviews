Key takeaways

BigCommerce mobile app builders let you go live with your own mobile app, for a fraction of the cost of a custom-built app. However, there are relatively few tools out there that work with BigCommerce sites, especially custom or enterprise-level stores. MobiLoud is a managed app service that turns your BigCommerce store into a custom mobile app while keeping your checkout, integrations and custom features.

If you run a BigCommerce store and you want a mobile app, the default doesn’t have to be a custom build with a several hundred thousand dollar bill attached.

BigCommerce doesn’t have a native, first-party feature that lets you launch a mobile app for your store, but there are some companies out there that do this for you. 

These range from low-cost no-code tools, to high-quality, service-driven platforms. All are focused on the idea that you can launch a mobile app for your BigCommerce store, without having to hire developers, and take on the cost and overhead associated with a traditional app build.

If you’re looking to build a mobile app, you’ve made one good choice. The next choice: the right BigCommerce mobile app builder to make it happen.

Keep reading and we’ll break down the best choices on the market.

What to Look For in a BigCommerce App Builder

BigCommerce stores tend to be more complex than the average ecommerce store: bigger catalogs, B2B requirements, multiple storefronts, headless builds.

This is important when it comes to launching a mobile app, because you want your app to be able to match that level of complexity.

Ask - does it sync with your full BigCommerce setup? Your Stencil theme (or a headless Catalyst frontend), your full catalog with custom fields, Customer Groups and Price Lists, your checkout, and your integrations. 

You should want your app to be an extension of your BigCommerce website. If it’s just a simple, watered-down version of your site, it’s hard to make the case for your customers to use it.

You might also need support for things like B2B edition, multi-storefront, or any other specific business features.

Make sure you find a tool that brings all the native features your customers are going to expect from a mobile app.

  • Native-feeling UI
  • App-exclusive pages and experiences
  • Push notifications
  • App store listings, one-tap loading from the home screen

In short, choose a tool that lets you launch a real app.

And finally, look at the company themselves, and what they provide for you. The kind of support and service you get, how they support you with the app, whether they help with app store submission, how they support you with growth, with maintenance, and all the extras that come with having a native app for your store.

The Quick Answer

MobiLoud designs, builds and manages native apps for BigCommerce stores while keeping your BigCommerce setup, Stencil or headless Catalyst, B2B Edition, Multi-Storefront, checkout and integrations, with no major rebuild required, and no second storefront to manage.

Alternatives include JMango360, a managed app builder that’s different architecturally, building an app that’s more distinct from your site. Shopgate, Unbound Commerce and Poq Commerce are more managed solutions geared towards legacy and enterprise brands. 

Elsewhere, you could consider a low-cost wrapper tool like Twinr, or (if you have a solid engineering team), configuring your own app with BigCommerce’s APIs and a low-code app builder.

The Top 7 Mobile App Builders for BigCommerce

Now let’s expand on the list of tools that let you turn your BigCommerce store into a native app.

For BigCommerce stores, the ecosystem of third-party tools and services is not as deep as for other platforms (Shopify in particular). If you do a search for ecommerce mobile app builders, for example, you’ll find that a lot are tied to Shopify, and won’t work if you’re on BigCommerce, full stop.

That said, there are options that work with your store; you may just need to do a little digging.

Without anything further, here are seven options to help you launch a high-quality mobile app, without the tax of a custom native build.

1. MobiLoud: For a complete app partner, and full feature parity with your store

Key features: App built from your existing BigCommerce storefront, fully managed build and launch, unlimited push notifications and a native abandoned-cart flow, deep linking, works with Stencil and headless Catalyst, carries B2B Edition and Multi-Storefront.

MobiLoud is a fully managed app builder that builds your brand’s own native iOS and Android apps from your BigCommerce storefront. The app updates with your site automatically, and you decide how close or different it looks. 

It’s one of the only ways to truly, reliably convert everything from your website into an app. Your Stencil theme (or a headless Catalyst frontend), your full catalog with custom fields, Customer Groups and Price Lists, your Optimized One-Page Checkout, your B2B Edition setup, Multi-Storefront, and your entire integration stack all carry over and power the app, which includes all the native features you need as well, such as push notifications, deep linking, and native navigation.

It’s architecturally flexible, because it works with any tech stack. No matter how custom your BigCommerce store, no matter how bespoke, it’ll carry over to your app without a problem.

MobiLoud backs all this up with complete, white-glove service to help you build and maintain your app, with minimal disruption to your team.

It’s a perfect fit for custom BigCommerce stores, or global brands with stores across different countries. Modere, a BigCommerce Enterprise brand operating across 10 markets, launched its app with MobiLoud in about four weeks.

Pricing starts from $799 a month, with no revenue share. It's a premium option, not the cheapest way to get an app, which is the honest tradeoff. MobiLoud holds a 4.7 out of 5 rating across 109 reviews on Capterra.

MobiLoud designs, builds and maintains your custom BigCommerce app, without adding a mobile development team.

2. JMango360: For a managed native app with B2B

Key features: Native iOS/Android with real-time BigCommerce sync, push notifications (back-in-stock, abandoned cart), native checkout via Braintree or Stripe, barcode scanner, B2B user groups and login-gated pricing, 40+ integrations.

JMango360 is a managed app builder with an official BigCommerce App Marketplace integration. Its plugin connects to the BigCommerce backend and syncs your catalog, inventory, payments, and shipping in real time, and the apps are native, built from your store's data through the integration. 

The model is done-for-you: you work through design and customization with JMango360's team and get a dedicated app success manager, with an app-management dashboard for running push, promotions, and content afterward.

It’s a natural fit for BigCommerce brands with B2B requirements. The platform supports user groups and prices behind login, so wholesale and trade customers see their own pricing in the app. It's also multi-platform (BigCommerce, Shopify, Adobe Commerce, and Salesforce Commerce Cloud), and is used by brands including Balmain, Leica, Subdued, and GLAMIRA. 

JMango360 doesn't publish current pricing upfront; it quotes each build through a demo. It holds a 4.8 out of 5 rating across 13 reviews on Capterra, and is listed in the BigCommerce App Marketplace.

3. Shopgate: For omnichannel retailers

Key features: Native apps with real-time sync, advanced push with segmentation, one-touch payments (Apple Pay, Google Pay), barcode scanner, analytics, and omnichannel features like Click and Reserve, Click and Collect, and ship-from-store.

Shopgate has been in mobile commerce since 2009, and it's less a lightweight app builder than a full mobile-commerce and omnichannel platform. For BigCommerce, it installs as a marketplace integration that connects Shopgate's commerce backend to your store and imports products, categories, and orders to power native iOS and Android apps. 

Shopgate handles the app-store submissions, OS updates, and maintenance, with a success team guiding the launch.

What distinguishes Shopgate is its omnichannel depth. Alongside the standard app features, it offers store-level inventory, Click and Collect, reserve-in-store, and ship-from-store, plus in-store clienteling tools, which makes it a strong fit for retailers that bridge physical and digital. 

It works with BigCommerce, Shopify, Magento, and Shopware, and is used by brands including Harbor Freight Tools, Alpha Industries, and Just Spices. Pricing is custom, with a starting point around €299 a month listed on G2. Shopgate holds a 4.0 out of 5 rating across 33 reviews on G2.

4. Unbound Commerce: For a fully custom app, B2C or B2B

Key features: Custom-built native iOS/Android apps, BigCommerce API integration with orders syncing back to your store, unlimited push, pre-integrated search, reviews, loyalty and chat add-ons, a content dashboard, and both B2C and B2B/wholesale apps.

Unbound Commerce is a US-based BigCommerce Certified Partner that builds custom native apps as a done-for-you project. Rather than a self-serve tool, you work with its team to design, build, and launch the app, and Unbound maintains it afterward. 

The build is native on the browsing and engagement screens, with cart and checkout running through customized versions of your storefront, and a server-side integration ties it to the BigCommerce Catalog API so orders flow back into your store.

It covers both B2C shopping apps and B2B/wholesale apps, which suits BigCommerce's strong B2B base, and it's been doing this since 2008, with apps for brands including Ashley Furniture, Rockport, New Balance, and Finish Line. 

Pricing is custom and project-based, with no revenue share, which Unbound positions as a lower total cost of ownership than tools that take a cut of sales. It holds a 4.56 out of 5 rating across 9 reviews on the BigCommerce App Marketplace.

5. PoqCommerce: For big retail brands that want a partner

Key features: Native app platform with a content CMS your team manages, push notifications, personalization and in-app loyalty, an analytics dashboard, proven app templates, and pre-built integrations.

Poq is an enterprise app-commerce platform built for large, recognizable retail brands. It delivers native iOS and Android apps from its platform (using proven templates drawn from its live apps, plus a CMS your team uses for day-to-day content), and connects to your commerce backend through its APIs. 

It works with BigCommerce via API integration, and Poq publishes a dedicated BigCommerce partner page; worth noting that its BigCommerce connection is through its flexible APIs rather than a purpose-built BigCommerce connector.

The real distinction is that Poq operates as a hands-on partner, not just software. Its team provides strategy, feature development, app marketing, and performance reviews, which is why it tends to suit big brands that want mobile run as a managed program. Its customer roster includes E.L.F. Beauty, Aeropostale, SNIPES, Kurt Geiger, and Hot Topic. 

Pricing is quote-only, with no public figures. Poq holds a 4.6 out of 5 rating across 10 reviews on G2.

6. Twinr: For a low-cost, self-serve app

Key features: Unlimited personalized push (abandoned cart, back-in-stock, low-stock), cart refresh, deep linking, real-time website sync with over-the-air updates, native in-app screens, and in-app messaging and analytics.

Twinr is a no-code, self-serve web-to-app converter. It turns your existing responsive BigCommerce storefront into native iOS and Android apps by wrapping the site in a native container, then adds an app layer on top: push, native navigation, deep linking, and app-only screens. 

Because it works on any responsive site, BigCommerce is supported by default rather than through a deep catalog-level integration. You build and publish the app yourself, with an optional done-for-you service if you'd rather hand it off.

Its pitch is retention at a low price. The core of the product is the engagement layer, unlimited push, abandoned-cart flows, and cart refresh that keeps app screens in step with the live cart, which makes it a sensible starting point for smaller stores that want an app without a big commitment. 

Plans start at $25 a month (or $19 billed annually) and go up to $599 a month for its highest tier, with a 14-day free trial.  Twinr holds a 4.5 out of 5 rating across 61 reviews on Trustpilot. 

7. Low-code builders and the BigCommerce API: For teams with developers

Key features: Visual app builders (FlutterFlow, Draftbit, Thunkable, Bravo Studio) that connect to BigCommerce's GraphQL Storefront API, Checkout SDK, and REST APIs, plus the headless route of wrapping a Catalyst storefront as a native app.

This last option isn't a single product, it's a route, and an honest one to know about. Low-code app platforms like FlutterFlow, Draftbit, Thunkable, and Bravo Studio can connect to BigCommerce's APIs and let you assemble an app on a visual canvas instead of writing everything from scratch. 

There's also a headless route: if you run a Catalyst (Next.js) storefront, a developer can package it as a progressive web app and wrap it natively with tools like Capacitor or PWABuilder.

None of these tools has a native BigCommerce connector. They offer generic REST or GraphQL data binding, so a developer still has to wire up authentication, catalog, cart, and checkout by hand.

This route can work if your team is already technical, and you have a lot of the expertise needed to build a mobile app in-house - but don’t want the depth of work needed to build a fully custom app from the ground up.

BigCommerce Mobile App Questions, Answered

Does BigCommerce have a mobile app builder? 

No - not a first-party mobile app builder. BigCommerce provides the headless architecture and APIs to help you ship a native app, but most of the custom development is still on you. To build a native app without hiring specialist developers, you’ll need to use one of the third-party tools listed above.

How much does a BigCommerce mobile app cost? 

It ranges widely. Self-serve web-to-app tools start around $25 a month, while more custom platforms could potentially be in the tens of thousands (though few of the platforms we listed show public pricing). MobiLoud sits in a nice sweet spot for mid-market and enterprise brands; pricing starting at $799 a month, which is much more cost-effective than a fully custom “from scratch” build.

Can I build a mobile app for a headless (Catalyst) BigCommerce store?

Yes. MobiLoud works with a headless Catalyst frontend seamlessly, letting you carry the same mobile-optimized design over to your mobile app.

Does B2B Edition work inside a mobile app?

It depends on the vendor. Some specifically support B2B edition; some custom app vendors will be able to work with this, but potentially at a higher cost, due to the higher complexity. With MobiLoud, anything that works on your site works in your app, so B2B edition is supported by default.

Are these native apps or just my website in an app? 

It varies by tool, and it's more of a spectrum than a yes-or-no. JMango360, Shopgate, and Poq build native app interfaces that pull from your store's data. MobiLoud and Unbound Commerce deliver native apps powered by your existing storefront, with native navigation and features layered on top, so you get the full app experience without rebuilding your store. Twinr takes a lighter approach, building an app from your responsive site, which is faster and cheaper to set up. What matters most for shoppers is whether the experience is fast and smooth, not the label.
