MobiLoud vs AppMySite: Which Website-to-App Solution Is Right for Your Business?

AppMySite is a do-it-yourself app maker with a free plan, for small businesses happy to build and manage a simple app themselves. MobiLoud builds a fully custom mobile app from your store. Everything on your site works in the app, there's no second store to manage, and our team builds and grows it with you.

Last reviewed: November 1, 2026

You can boost customer engagement and brand presence by converting your website into a mobile app. The question is... how?

Two popular solutions for this transformation are MobiLoud and AppMySite. While both platforms enable businesses to create mobile apps from existing websites, they take different approaches that suit varying business needs and priorities.

This article compares MobiLoud and AppMySite to help you determine which platform better fits your business requirements, and to see when MobiLoud might be the best alternative.

Want to jump ahead and discuss your project with a project manager? Book a free consultation now.

Overview: MobiLoud vs AppMySite

MobiLoud is a fully-managed website to app service for mid-market and enterprise ecommerce brands.

Instead of building and configuring the app yourself, MobiLoud's team builds it for you, on top of your existing tech stack.

Your existing website becomes the foundation for a high-performance app, with all functionality intact, plus native features such as push notifications, native navigation, splash screens, and deep linking.

It supports any ecommerce platform, including Shopify, BigCommerce, Adobe Commerce (Magento), WooCommerce, and even headless or fully custom sites.

AppMySite is a no-code, automated app builder designed for quick website-to-app conversion, mainly for WordPress and WooCommerce sites.

It follows a self-service approach: you create an app by entering your website URL and configuring it through their interface, with a live preview to see changes in real-time.

It's a lot cheaper, and what you get is very different.

Key Differences Between MobiLoud and AppMySite

MobiLoud and AppMySite both convert your website into a mobile app.

But that's about the extent of the overlap. Realistically, there's little direct competition between them. One is a cost-effective DIY tool. The other is a premium app development service.

Let's take a closer look at the differences now.

1. Service Model: Done-for-You vs. Do-It-Yourself

MobiLoud operates as a fully managed service.

You don’t need to use a dashboard or write code. MobiLoud’s team handles the entire app build, from configuration and native feature integration to quality assurance, App Store submissions, push notification setup, and more.

AppMySite requires you to build the app yourself. You enter your site URL, adjust settings, customize branding, and manage the store submission process. This self-service model is user-friendly but needs your time, attention, and some technical confidence.

2. Strategic Support: Dedicated Experts vs. General Help

MobiLoud customers work with a dedicated team of app specialists who provide strategic guidance, technical support, quality assurance, and post-launch growth assistance.

MobiLoud provides launch guidance and ongoing customer success support. On Enterprise, MobiLoud also provides a dedicated success manager, Slack access and recurring strategy and performance reviews.

AppMySite offers email and chat support to assist with platform use.

3. Feature Retention & Flexibility

MobiLoud designs your custom app on your existing website, keeping your ecommerce flows, user accounts, content and integrations, with native navigation and push notifications.

AppMySite works well with WordPress and WooCommerce sites. For other platforms, it uses webviews that maintain content but may not provide native-level integrations.

4. Maintenance & Updates

With MobiLoud, updates made to your website automatically show up in the app. No need to manage content separately.

MobiLoud also handles operating system updates, SDK changes, app store resubmissions, and ongoing maintenance to keep your app compliant and current.

AppMySite syncs content from supported platforms, but app maintenance, including submissions and compliance, is the user's responsibility.

5. Platform Compatibility

MobiLoud is platform-agnostic, supporting Shopify, WooCommerce, BigCommerce, Adobe Commerce, Salesforce Commerce Cloud, headless builds, and custom stacks.

AppMySite primarily supports WordPress and WooCommerce. Other platforms are supported via webviews with fewer native integrations.

6. Long-Term Value

MobiLoud offers more than just app creation. It provides a mobile retention and revenue channel managed by experts, including:

  • Rapid launch timelines
  • Full lifecycle management
  • No duplication of effort
  • Ongoing push notification strategy and analytics support
  • No revenue share or transaction fees, one clear monthly price

AppMySite is a cost-effective entry point for small businesses or individual operators comfortable managing their own app growth and maintenance.

When to Choose MobiLoud Over AppMySite

If you want an affordable, entry-level option to launch an app for your WordPress site, AppMySite is a suitable starting point.

Choose MobiLoud if:

  • You want to launch a polished app fast without adding to your development workload.
  • Your site includes custom features, login systems, payment flows, or third-party integrations.
  • You lack the technical resources or time to manage a DIY build.
  • You want push notifications to drive engagement and ROI, with expert help to set them up and optimize.
  • You value reliability, expert support, and peace of mind over self-configuration.

MobiLoud means a lot less work for your team, with greater long-term impact than DIY tools.

Conclusion: Making Your Decision

While AppMySite offers an accessible, budget-friendly option for website owners (especially those on WordPress), MobiLoud provides a full, hands-off solution that preserves 100% of your website's functionality with expert support throughout the process.

Choose MobiLoud if:

  • Your website has complex or custom functionality
  • You lack technical resources or time for DIY app development
  • You need expert guidance and ongoing support
  • You require real-time synchronization
  • Your website is built on a platform other than WordPress
  • You want an app that grows with your business

Choose AppMySite if:

  • You have a WordPress or WooCommerce site with standard functionality
  • You're comfortable with a DIY approach
  • You're working with a limited budget
  • You have time to learn and manage the platform
  • You need a quick solution to establish an app presence

For businesses prioritizing full functionality retention, expert support, and minimal hands-on management, MobiLoud's full-service approach justifies the investment through time savings and peace of mind.

MobiLoud provides a mobile development team on retainer, at a fraction of the cost of hiring a typical development agency.
