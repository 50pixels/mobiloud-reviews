MobiLoud: A Higher Upside Hulk Mobile App Builder Alternative

Hulk Mobile App Builder is a low-cost Shopify app builder with plans from $79 a month, for smaller stores that want to launch an app quickly on their own. MobiLoud builds a fully custom mobile app from your store. Everything on your site works in the app, there's no second store to manage, and our team builds and grows it with you.

Last reviewed: November 1, 2026

Hulk Mobile App Builder

  • Simple, user-friendly mobile app builder.
  • Affordable pricing, good entry-level option.
  • Easier alternative to custom development.
  • Uses pre-built blocks and templates.
  • Focused integration library.
  • Requires managing app separately from website (requiring separate management).

MobiLoud

  • Everything from your website works in your app, with no rebuilding.
  • Fully done-for-you service (including maintenance and app store submission).
  • App updates automatically with your site (no extra work or maintenance).
  • More expensive.

MobiLoud is a great fit if you

  • Have a mobile-optimized website that already provides a great user experience.
  • Rely on specific features, elements or integrations on your website.
  • Want to save time and energy building and maintaining your app.

Why MobiLoud is different

MobiLoud builds your brand's own app from your store, so there's nothing to rebuild.

Unlike drag-and-drop builders, MobiLoud is a done-for-you service that transforms what you already built for the web into native apps.

You'll keep all the features from your website (plugins, apps, integrations, custom features, A/B tested optimizations) and add all the best features of native apps, including native UI and push notifications.

MobiLoud works with every ecommerce platform, including Shopify, Shopify Plus, and headless builds.

With MobiLoud, your app fully syncs with your website. Whenever you make a change to your website, your app updates automatically, leaving you with only one platform to manage, and little to no additional overhead.

Why MobiLoud is the best alternative

MobiLoud comes with more flexibility and less overhead than Hulk Mobile App Builder (and other drag-and-drop app builders).

You're not constrained by pre-built blocks, templates and integrations.

Everything from your website works in your app, out of the box.

With MobiLoud, you have full flexibility. Anything you can do on your website, you can do in the app as well.

All of this comes with push notifications that bring shoppers back and drive sales and engagement in just a few clicks.

The best part is the time and effort you save.

No rebuilding. No tinkering. No work.

We build your apps, publish them to the app stores, and take care of maintenance and updates after you launch.

You continue to manage just one platform (your website), while spreading your reach to millions of potential app users, with no addition to your workflow.

Frequently asked questions

How much does MobiLoud cost?+

MobiLoud starts at $799 per month. We agree the scope and price with you before work starts.

The subscription covers the work our team does to build and publish your apps.

The cost is a little higher than Hulk upfront, but over time, we'll save you money by reducing the amount of labor required to manage your apps.

We also put more flexibility on the revenue potential of your app, so the ROI of building an app with MobiLoud often turns out to be far superior.

How long does it take?+

It typically takes up to 4 weeks (potentially less) to launch your apps.

This includes the time it takes for our developers to code the native elements in your app, and integrate your website with the apps.

We thoroughly test the apps before launch, and you'll also have the opportunity to test it yourself and provide feedback.

At the end of it all, we handle the app store submission process for you.

All that takes 2 to 4 weeks, and then your own mobile apps are live, in the app stores and on your customers' devices.

Will it work with my website?+

Yes!

No matter your tech stack, MobiLoud will work with it.

Shopify, Shopify Plus, Salesforce Commerce Cloud, Magento, BigCommerce, even custom websites... MobiLoud works perfectly.

It's platform-agnostic. We're not tied to any specific platform. If you migrate to a new platform, or add custom features to your website, your MobiLoud apps will keep working with no disruption.

Are MobiLoud apps native apps?+

While the content within your apps is shared with your website, the apps themselves are built with native code.

Your users will be able to download the apps and load them on their mobile devices, just like any native app.

You'll be able to send native push notifications, not mobile web notifications like a website or a PWA.

From your customers' perspective, they'll be using an app that looks and feels like a custom native app; with none of the work, cost, time investment and overhead for you.

Do I need to know how to code?+

Not at all!

While your apps will be built with native code, the experience from your end is fully no-code.

Our developers handle all the technical parts, while the content within your app is managed from your website.

If you want to add a new feature, change the design, make a tweak, just do so through your web platform, and your apps update automatically.
