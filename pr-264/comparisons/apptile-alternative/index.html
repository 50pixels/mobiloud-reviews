Why brands build their app with MobiLoud

With MobiLoud, your Shopify store becomes a custom native app, and your team keeps managing one store instead of two.

Your store, transformed into a custom native app

MobiLoud builds your app from your existing Shopify store and designs it around your brand, with native navigation, app-only screens and a home screen built for the app.

You get a custom native app without starting a second store from scratch.

One store to manage, two channels to sell through

Publish a collection, change a price or start a promotion in Shopify, and your site and your app both have it straight away. Your team keeps one workflow, one set of content and one store to maintain.

Your full store, ready at launch

Reviews, loyalty, subscriptions, search, bundles and your checkout are in the app from day one, along with anything custom your developers have built. If you offer live shopping on your site, that comes with it too.

Native features, and custom ones when you need them

Push notifications through OneSignal or Klaviyo, native navigation, saved sign-in and app-only offers all come with your app.

When you want more, our team scopes and builds custom features such as Face ID, barcode scanning or location tools.

A team that stays with you

We build and publish your app, keep it updated for every iOS and Android release, and work with you after launch on installs, push campaigns and growing the app’s share of your sales.

Apptile vs MobiLoud: FAQs

Have any more questions we didn’t answer? Book a call or send us a message to clear it all up.

Does Apptile work outside Shopify?

Apptile is built for Shopify and Shopify Plus stores, and installs as a Shopify app. MobiLoud works with Shopify and with any other platform, including Magento, BigCommerce, WooCommerce and custom builds.

Can I sell live with MobiLoud?

MobiLoud doesn’t have its own live selling feature. If you offer live shopping on your website, it works in your MobiLoud app like the rest of your store. If you want live selling built into the app and streamed to your social channels, Apptile is built for it.

Which is cheaper, Apptile or MobiLoud?

Apptile is cheaper to start. Its Plus plan is $599 a month and Enterprise $1,199, each with a set number of monthly active users and live-stream hours, and usage fees above them. MobiLoud starts at $799 a month, which covers the design, the build, the launch and a team that keeps your app updated.

Which is faster to launch?

Apptile is faster to launch, at about 10 days by its own estimate. MobiLoud apps usually go live in 2 to 4 weeks, including the store reviews, because our team designs, builds and tests the app for you.

Is a MobiLoud app a native app?

A MobiLoud app is a native iOS and Android app, built in Swift and Kotlin. It uses a mix of your existing web code and custom elements built for the app, such as native navigation, push notifications and login, and customers get an app that looks and feels like any other on their phone.

Will my Shopify apps work in a MobiLoud app?

Your Shopify apps work in a MobiLoud app from day one. Reviews, loyalty, subscriptions, search, bundles and any other app on your store are in your app at launch, so there’s no integrations list to check.

Can I move my Apptile app to MobiLoud without losing my users?

You can move your Apptile app to MobiLoud without losing your users. If the app is in your own Apple and Google developer accounts, we publish the new app as an update to that listing. If it’s in Apptile’s account, Apptile moves it to yours on request first. Either way, your users get a normal update, and your ratings and reviews stay.

Do I have to build and maintain the app myself with MobiLoud?

You don’t build or maintain the app yourself with MobiLoud. We design and build it, publish it to both stores and update it for every new iOS and Android release, while your team keeps working on your store.
