Tapcart vs MobiLoud: The Two Leading Mobile App Builders for Shopify Stores

Tapcart gives you an editor to build your app as a second storefront. MobiLoud builds your app on your live website, so everything carries over, and your site and app always stay in sync.

Last reviewed: November 1, 2026

Tapcart vs MobiLoud in 30 Seconds

Tapcart is the better fit if you

  • Want to design the app yourself, in a drag-and-drop editor
  • Want the app to look and work differently from your website
  • Want to launch within a few weeks
  • Want to start for less than $1,000 a month
  • Want push content creation and personalization built in

MobiLoud is the better fit if you

  • Want everything on your website to work in the app
  • Want to make each change once, on your site
  • Are on a custom or headless Shopify build, Shopify Plus, or any non-Shopify platform
  • Want a team to build, launch and look after the app
  • Have custom features or apps a builder can’t recreate

How they work

One storefront, or two

Tapcart works the way no-code app builders do. It links up with your store via Shopify’s API, and you build the app itself from blocks, in a separate editor. You build and maintain two separate user experiences in parallel.

MobiLoud starts from your website instead. Your site powers the app, and we add the native parts on top: tab navigation, push notifications and your App Store listing. You can build app-only pages and offer exclusive merchandising and promos for app users, but you control everything through your existing website.

You want to add a custom feature

You build it on your site and set it to show only in the app. Any testing platform you already use on your site works on it too.

Platforms

Tapcart

Tapcart works with Shopify stores only, on any paid Shopify plan. Some features need Shopify Plus.

It supports Shopify Markets and Shopify B2B. Headless Shopify storefronts can use its SDKs for cart and wishlist sync, with checkout on Shopify.

MobiLoud

MobiLoud works with any website: Shopify and Shopify Plus, Magento and Adobe Commerce, BigCommerce, Salesforce Commerce Cloud, WooCommerce, Shopware, and custom or headless builds.

What it means for youIf you’re on Shopify and plan to stay there, both will work. If you’re on another platform, or might move later, Tapcart isn’t an option.

Design and editing

Tapcart

You design the app in App Studio, Tapcart’s drag-and-drop editor, from templates and blocks, or by describing changes to its editor. Changes go live without an app store review, and you can roll them back from the version history.

Developers can write custom blocks in React. Writing custom block code needs Tapcart’s Enterprise+ plan.

MobiLoud

Your app starts from your website’s design, so there’s nothing to recreate. We add native navigation, such as a tab bar and menus, and design the icon, launch screen and store listing with you.

You can build app-only screens, which are created and managed through your web platform, and you can show or hide certain elements specifically for the app.

What it means for youIf you want to design the app yourself and change it often, Tapcart’s editor is the easier option. If you want the app to update with your site automatically, without keeping two designs in step, MobiLoud is.

Checkout

Tapcart

Checkout opens your store’s own Shopify checkout inside the app, with Shop Pay, Apple Pay and the other payment methods you offer.

MobiLoud

The app uses the checkout on your website, which works normally inside the app. Shop Pay, Apple Pay, and all your checkout apps and customizations work just as they do on your site.

What it means for youFor standard Shopify checkouts, there’s no difference between the two. MobiLoud has fewer limitations though, which matters if you have a highly custom checkout experience.

Integrations and your apps

Tapcart

Tapcart lists 87 supported integrations, including Klaviyo, Attentive, Recharge, Skio, Yotpo, Okendo, Judge.me, LoyaltyLion, Gorgias, Rebuy and Nosto.

It may be possible to integrate other tools, though this will require custom development, and a higher cost.

MobiLoud

Any app or script that works on your website works in the app, including ones you built yourself.

That means we work with virtually any Shopify app, third-party integration, custom feature, and anything else that already works on your site.

What it means for youTapcart supports a good number of popular apps, but not all. If you want everything from your site to carry over to your app, MobiLoud is the best way to do it.

Push notifications

Tapcart

Push is built into your app and sent from your Tapcart dashboard with unlimited sends, automated flows such as welcome, abandoned cart and win-back, segments and an in-app inbox. It can also go through Klaviyo, Attentive or Braze.

MobiLoud

Push works through native integrations with OneSignal, or Klaviyo on Business and Enterprise, plus built-in abandoned cart sequences, welcome flows and inactive user flows.

MobiLoud also helps you with push setup and ongoing guidance on getting the most out of the channel.

What it means for youBoth platforms give you full access to native push notifications. Tapcart’s is managed in their own dashboard, while MobiLoud uses a separate push provider.

Content and personalization

Tapcart

Tapcart’s add-on writes and sends push notifications, suggests new app content from your site, and makes product imagery and short videos. Its For You feed personalizes collections for each shopper.

MobiLoud

The search and product recommendations on your website work in the app too.

Your custom pages, chat and personalized experiences carry over from your site.

What it means for youTapcart includes app-specific content creation and personalization. With MobiLoud, you keep the experiences you already offer on your website.

Analytics

Tapcart

Tapcart has its own dashboard for app revenue, retention and push. Scale and Enterprise+ add advanced analytics, A/B tests and heatmaps. Events can also go to GA4, Firebase and AppsFlyer.

MobiLoud

The app works with whatever web analytics platforms you have. We tag app traffic so it shows up alongside the rest of your analytics, and we add Firebase for native app data.

What it means for youTapcart has more analytics built in. MobiLoud is more flexible, because it works with the analytics platforms you already use.

Who does the work

Tapcart

Your team designs and manages the app in Tapcart’s dashboard. Tapcart submits your first build, and support is available around the clock. Enterprise+ adds an account manager, developer support and a Slack channel.

MobiLoud

We build the app, publish it to both stores and update it for every new iOS and Android version. We help you plan the launch and set up push.

On Enterprise, MobiLoud also provides a dedicated success manager, regular strategy reviews and a Slack or Teams channel with our team.

What it means for youWith Tapcart, someone on your team owns the app. With MobiLoud, we do most of that work with you.

Time to launch

Tapcart

Tapcart says most apps launch 1 to 3 weeks from sign-up. Launch speed can drag out if you need to build custom features that Tapcart’s basic features don’t support.

MobiLoud

Most apps go live in 2 to 4 weeks, including the Apple and Google reviews. We build, test and submit the app, and you review it along the way.

What it means for youTapcart is usually quicker to launch. MobiLoud’s process takes a little longer, but needs little of your time.

Owning the app

Tapcart

Your app is published under your own Apple and Google developer accounts, with admin access for Tapcart.

MobiLoud

Your app is published under your own Apple and Google developer accounts, so the listing, reviews and users are yours.

What it means for youBoth put the app in your accounts, so either way you keep your listing and your users if you move later.

Total cost of ownership

The monthly price is only part of what an app costs you. The rest is the time your team spends on it, and any custom work it needs.

With Tapcart, someone on your team designs the app, builds each screen and keeps it in step with your website. Promotions, page redesigns and new features on your site all have to be set up again in App Studio. With MobiLoud, those changes carry over from your site, and our team handles the build, the updates and the store submissions.

Custom features are the other big cost. Anything Tapcart’s blocks and integrations don’t cover needs a custom block, and writing custom block code needs Tapcart’s Enterprise+ plan, from $2,850 a month, plus a developer to build it. With MobiLoud, the features already on your site work in the app, so there’s nothing to rebuild.

If your budget for an app is under about $1,000 a month, Tapcart is still the practical choice. Above that, compare the total cost, not just the monthly price.

Tapcart vs MobiLoud: FAQs

Have any more questions we didn’t answer? Book a call or send us a message to clear it all up.

Is Tapcart only for Shopify?

Yes. Tapcart works with Shopify stores only, on any paid Shopify plan. If your store is on Magento, BigCommerce, Salesforce Commerce Cloud, WooCommerce or a custom platform, Tapcart isn’t an option. MobiLoud works with all of them, and with Shopify too.

Which is cheaper, Tapcart or MobiLoud?

Tapcart. Its plans start at $500 a month, while MobiLoud starts at $799 a month. The two prices pay for different things, though. Tapcart’s pays for the software, and your team builds and manages the app with it. Ours pays for the build, the launch and a team that keeps the app updated.

Which is faster to launch?

Usually Tapcart. It says most apps launch 1 to 3 weeks from sign-up. MobiLoud apps usually go live in 2 to 4 weeks, including the Apple and Google reviews, because our team builds and tests the app for you.

Will my Shopify apps work in a MobiLoud app?

Yes. The app is powered by your website, so reviews, loyalty, subscriptions, search, bundles and any other app or script on your store work in the app the way they work on your site. There’s no integrations list to check.

Can I move my Tapcart app to MobiLoud without losing my users?

Yes. Your app is in your own Apple and Google developer accounts, so we publish the new app as an update to your existing listing. Your users get it as a normal app update, and your ratings and reviews stay. We time the switch so the new version is live before you cancel Tapcart.

Can I still have app-only content and promotions with MobiLoud?

Yes. App-only offers, collections and screens are built on your website and shown only in the app, so you manage them where you manage everything else. You can also send app-only offers by push.

Do I have to build and maintain the app myself with MobiLoud?

No. We build the app, publish it to the App Store and Google Play, and update it for every new iOS and Android version and store policy change. Your team keeps working on your website, and the app follows.

Which is better for a Shopify Plus brand?

It depends on what you want from the app. If you want your team to design an app that works differently from your site, and your apps are all on Tapcart’s integrations list, Tapcart fits. If your store is heavily customized, you want the app to keep up with it automatically, or you want a team to manage the app for you, MobiLoud fits better.
