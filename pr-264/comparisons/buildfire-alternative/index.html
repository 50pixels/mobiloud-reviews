Buildfire vs MobiLoud: Two Different Ways to Build Your App

Buildfire is a no-code builder for standalone apps designed from scratch, and suits churches, gyms, events and internal tools more than online stores. MobiLoud builds a fully custom mobile app from your store. Everything on your site works in the app, there's no second store to manage, and our team builds and grows it with you.

Last reviewed: November 1, 2026

If you're comparing Buildfire and MobiLoud, you need to know that they offer different ways to build and maintain an app. Both let you launch Android and iOS apps, without hiring developers. But the use cases for each are vastly different.

Buildfire is a traditional no-code tool. You sign up, log in, and build an app from a blank canvas using their drag-and-drop editor and plugin marketplace. You can pay a few hundred bucks per month, and launch a good, functional mobile app.

MobiLoud is a managed app development service, made to help mid-market and enterprise ecommerce brands launch mobile apps built from their existing tech stack.

The MobiLoud team designs, builds and manages your app, which is fully synchronized with your web store. It costs more (starting at $799 per month), but the cost is to cover the level of service and hands-on work provided.

The right question isn't which one is better. It's what you're building.

That determines whether Buildfire or MobiLoud is right for you.

What is Buildfire?

Buildfire is a no-code app builder that lets you create native iOS and Android apps using a drag-and-drop editor and a marketplace of plugins. 

It’s suitable for a wide range of industries: churches, fitness, events, real estate, education, restaurants, construction, healthcare, and ecommerce.

You pick a template or start from scratch, add features through their plugin marketplace, customize the design, and publish to the app stores. Buildfire also offers professional services where their team designs and builds the app for you.

For ecommerce, Buildfire can connect to Shopify to pull in your product catalog. But the app itself is going to be a standalone product. You're designing screens, configuring layouts, and managing content inside Buildfire's platform, separate from your website.

Buildfire pricing

  • Standard: $209/month (basic publishing, 100K push notifications)
  • Growth: $389/month (one in-app purchase, account manager)
  • Scale: $549/month (unlimited purchases, premium support)
  • Professional Services: custom pricing

Who Buildfire is for

Businesses that want to build a standalone mobile app from scratch. Buildfire is strongest for non-ecommerce use cases (churches, fitness studios, events, internal tools) but has ecommerce capabilities through Shopify integration and in-app purchase features.

It's a good fit if you don't have an existing website to work from, or if you want an app experience that's intentionally different and independent from your website.

What is MobiLoud?

MobiLoud is a premium managed service for mid-market and enterprise ecommerce brands that want to turn their websites into native mobile apps.

MobiLoud is not a DIY platform. There's no drag-and-drop editor, no templates, no plugin marketplace for you to wire up. We do the work: native iOS and Android engineering, app design matched to your brand, push notification setup (OneSignal, or Klaviyo on Business and Enterprise), GA4 and Firebase analytics, cross-device QA, App Store and Google Play submission, ongoing maintenance, and push strategy after launch.

Your team's job is to give brand assets, approve the build, and keep managing your website like always. Our approach ensures everything from your website carries over to your app, and the app and website always stay fully in sync, reducing the operational lift that comes with launching a mobile app.

MobiLoud works with any ecommerce platform: Shopify, BigCommerce, Magento, WooCommerce, Salesforce Commerce Cloud, Shopware, and custom builds.

MobiLoud pricing

MobiLoud starts at $799 per month. The cost includes the app build, app store management, QA, and ongoing support and maintenance.

Who MobiLoud is for

Established ecommerce brands, doing a minimum of seven figures in annual online revenue, and already have a strong, fast, mobile-friendly website.

Managing a mid-market or enterprise ecommerce brand?

If you already have a website generating real revenue, MobiLoud can turn it into a native iOS and Android app, with your full checkout, integrations, and customer experience intact. Our team handles everything: build, app store publishing, and ongoing maintenance.

Tool vs Service: The Core Difference Between MobiLoud and Buildfire

The clearest way to understand how Buildfire and MobiLoud differ is to look at two questions: what each product is, and what happens day-to-day after you sign up.

What you're buying

Buildfire is software. You're paying for access to a platform that lets you build and operate an app yourself. The product is the platform.

MobiLoud is a service. You're paying for a team to design, build, and operate a custom native app for you. The product is the app, and the team behind it.

This shows up in pricing. Buildfire ranges from $209 to $549 per month, in line with self-serve software pricing. MobiLoud starts at $799/month, pricing that reflects that a team is doing the work end to end.

Where the app comes from

Buildfire starts from a blank canvas. You pick a template or start from zero, drag in screens and components, configure plugins, customize design, and publish. The app is something you create inside Buildfire's platform.

MobiLoud starts from your existing website. Your site is the content engine, the design language, the checkout, and the integration layer. The native iOS and Android apps are layered on top: we handle the native engineering, navigation, push infrastructure, and integrations with your stack. You don't build anything inside our platform, because there's no platform for you to log into.

Day-to-day operation

With Buildfire, you operate the platform. Adding products, updating content, changing layouts, configuring new features. Their team supports you, but the day-to-day is yours.

With MobiLoud, your team operates your website like always. Whatever changes on the site flows through to the app automatically. Our team handles app-specific work: platform updates, App Store policy changes, third-party integration changes, push strategy, and ongoing maintenance.

This is the most concrete way the two products diverge. Buildfire customers manage their app. MobiLoud customers manage their website.

Ecommerce specifically

Buildfire can connect to Shopify to pull in your product catalog. For ecommerce cases where the app is the primary surface (a restaurant app, a small grocery store, a fitness studio's shop) that integration covers a lot of ground.

MobiLoud is built for ecommerce websites. Because the app uses your actual website, every tool in your stack works in the app: your review provider, loyalty program, search, personalization engine, subscription manager, custom checkout extensions, and any third-party Shopify app you rely on. The checkout in the app is your website's checkout, not an API-rebuilt version.

For ecommerce brands with complex stacks and meaningful revenue on the line, that's the dividing line. For simpler ecommerce use cases where a product feed and a clean storefront cover the requirement, Buildfire's integration works fine.

Scope

Buildfire serves dozens of industries: churches, fitness, events, real estate, education, restaurants, construction, healthcare, ecommerce. Breadth is the platform's strength.

MobiLoud is built only for ecommerce brands with a website, plus a small number of adjacent web-first categories (publishers, online communities). We don't build apps for non-web businesses, and we don't build standalone apps. Anything that requires in-app purchases (digital subscriptions, virtual currency, ad-removal upgrades inside the app) is outside our scope and inside Buildfire's.

Which One Should You Choose?

If you're building a new app from a blank canvas, Buildfire is built for that. If you're looking to build a mobile app for a 7+ figure ecommerce website, MobiLoud is built for that. Realistically, there's not a lot of overlap.

Buildfire is the right product if:

  • You don't have an existing website, or you want an app that's intentionally separate from your website
  • Your use case is non-ecommerce: church, fitness studio, event, real estate, education, healthcare, internal tool
  • You want to operate the app yourself with a no-code platform
  • You need in-app purchases for digital subscriptions, virtual goods, or premium content
  • A Shopify integration that pulls product data is sufficient for your ecommerce needs, and you don't need the rest of your website's tech stack in the app

MobiLoud is the right product if:

  • You have an established ecommerce website doing real revenue and you want to extend it to native apps
  • Your website uses a stack of third-party tools (reviews, loyalty, subscriptions, search, CRO) that all need to just work in the app
  • You want your team focused on the website while a separate team handles the app
  • The app needs to update with the website automatically: products, checkout, promotions and content
  • You're on a platform other than Shopify (BigCommerce, Magento, Salesforce Commerce Cloud, WooCommerce, headless, custom) and need an app that supports it natively

Frequently asked questions

Does MobiLoud work with my website?+

Yes!

MobiLoud works with all web platforms, including Shopify, WordPress, Salesforce Commerce Cloud, Magento/Adobe Commerce, and any other platform you can think of.

We even work with headless CMS and custom builds. There are no restrictions to the kind of website you can turn into an app.

How much work is it?+

With MobiLoud, it's zero work.

As long as your website is mobile-friendly, it's already 90% of the way to being a native app.

Our engineers do all the work to bring your app to life. You can give your input, and test and give feedback on your apps, or just wait until your apps go live (typically within two to four weeks).

Can I convert my website into a mobile app?+

With Buildfire? Not exactly.

For a start, you'll need to rebuild your app UI and UX within their platform.

You may be able to integrate your app with the backend of your website, if you're on a popular website platform like Shopify or WordPress.

But it's still not a straightforward conversion process.

With MobiLoud? Yes.

That's exactly what we're made to do. As long as your website is already mobile-friendly, it's super easy to get an app built from it.

There's no effort required from you. You get your own native app for iOS and Android, built from your website and updated with it automatically.

What does the MobiLoud process cover?+

We handle everything to do with building your app from your website.

We write the native code that allows your website to work in mobile environments. We handle testing, QA, revisions, and app store submission.

Once you're live, we'll handle all technical maintenance and updates for your app, while you continue to focus on your website.

Is MobiLoud a no-code app builder?+

MobiLoud designs, builds and maintains your app on your existing website.

We're a fully managed service, dedicated to helping you navigate the world of mobile apps, and ensure your app is a success.

Think of us more like an app development service, only closer to the price of an app builder than a typical agency.
