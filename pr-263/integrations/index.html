MobiLoud Integrations

If it works on your site,
it works in your app

MobiLoud builds native iOS and Android apps powered by your live website, so the search, reviews, loyalty programs and checkout your customers already use automatically work in your app.

How it works

Your website is the integration

We build your app on your live website. Everything that works on your site works in the app, with nothing extra to connect, set up or keep in sync.

  • Every app and integration

    Reviews, loyalty, subscriptions, search, chat and quizzes work in your app the way they work on your site, with the same settings and the same data.

  • Every plugin and extension

    The Shopify apps, WordPress plugins and Magento extensions you’ve installed come with your site. There’s no separate app version to find or set up.

  • Your theme and custom code

    Your theme, custom sections, scripts and the features your developers built carry over as they are.

  • Any platform

    Shopify, Magento, WooCommerce, BigCommerce, Salesforce, headless or built in-house. If it runs in a mobile browser, we can build your app on it.

Native integrations

Need something native? We’ll build it in.

Your app isn’t just a repackaging of your website. We build native integrations for push notifications, analytics and more app-only features, and can also build custom SDK integrations as you need them.

  • OneSignal

    Push notifications

    Send app push from your own OneSignal account, with your segments and automations.

  • Klaviyo

    Push notifications

    On Business and Enterprise, add app push to your Klaviyo flows and campaigns, next to email and SMS.

  • Salesforce Marketing Cloud

    Push notifications

    On Enterprise, send app push from Marketing Cloud, alongside the rest of your messaging.

  • Firebase & GA4

    App analytics

    See app users and sessions under your own Google account, kept apart from web traffic.

  • AppsFlyer

    Ad attribution

    Tie installs and in-app revenue back to the Meta and Google campaigns that drove them.

  • Adjust

    Ad attribution

    Measure which campaigns bring in app installs and the revenue that follows.

  • Apple, Google & Facebook

    Sign-in

    Customers sign in with the accounts they already use, handled natively in the app.

Custom SDK integrations

On Enterprise, we can build custom integrations or native features, such as Face ID and biometric login, barcode scanners, location features and more.

Ask about a feature

MobiLoud vs app builders

Keep every integration

Other app builders need a dedicated integration for every service you use on your site. This limits what you can carry over, and they don’t always work the same way in your app. With MobiLoud, everything works, out of the box.

No-code app builders

  • You rebuild your store in their editor, screen by screen
  • Only carries over services on their integrations list
  • Custom dev needed for anything not on the list
  • Leave your theme and custom features on the web
  • Need each new integration set up again in the app

MobiLoud

  • Your app is powered by your live site, not APIs
  • Works with every integration, app and plugin on your site
  • Carries over your theme and custom features
  • New integrations go live in your app automatically
  • Custom SDK integrations and native features when you need them

Integrations: FAQs

Have any more questions we didn't answer? Book a 30-minute call or send us a message to clear it all up.

Book a call
Will the integrations on my website work in the app?

Yes. Your app is built on your live website, so every integration, app, plugin and script that runs on your site runs in the app too. Reviews, loyalty, subscriptions, search, chat, pop-ups and your checkout work the way they do on the web, with the same settings and the same data.

Why doesn’t MobiLoud have a list of supported integrations?

Because we don’t need one. App builders rebuild your store inside their own platform, so each service needs a connector on their side, and their list shows which connectors they’ve built. Your MobiLoud app runs your website, so an integration works in the app as soon as it works on your site.

Do I need to set anything up for my integrations to work in the app?

Likely no. There’s nothing to connect or configure for the integrations on your website, and your team keeps managing them where it always has. The only setup is for native features like push notifications, and our team takes care of that while we build your app.

Which native integrations does MobiLoud support?

MobiLoud builds native integrations into your app for push notifications, analytics, and basic native features like social logins. We can also build custom native integrations, as well as native features like Face ID, scoped and quoted on a case-by-case basis.

If I add a new integration to my website later, will it work in the app?

Almost certainly, as soon as it’s live on your site. Your app loads your website, so new apps, plugins and changes reach the app straight away. There’s no app update to ship and nothing for us to connect.

Will my checkout and payment options work in the app?

Yes. Your app uses the checkout on your website, so your payment methods, discount codes, checkout apps and customizations work the way they do on the web. Google Pay is the one that needs a small change, because Google doesn’t let it run inside an app’s web view, so we set it up to open in a secure pop-up instead.

Will my analytics platforms count app visits?

Yes. Any analytics service, tag or pixel that runs on your site runs in the app too, so app visits show up in the reports you already use. We also set up Firebase and Google Analytics 4 under your own Google account, so you can look at app users on their own, separately from your web traffic.

Does Klaviyo work in the app?

Yes, in two ways. Klaviyo’s onsite tracking runs in your app the way it does on your site, so app activity feeds the same profiles, flows and segments. On Business and Enterprise, MobiLoud’s native Klaviyo integration also lets you send app push from your Klaviyo flows and campaigns, next to your email and SMS.

Do the integrations I use need to support mobile apps?

No. They only need to work on your mobile site, which they almost certainly already do. Your app runs your website, so an integration that works for shoppers on their phone’s browser works in the app.

Will my custom features and custom code work in the app?

Yes. Your app runs the same code as your website, so the features your developers built, your theme customizations and your custom scripts work in the app as they are. Nothing has to be rebuilt for the app, and anything your team changes later reaches the app too.

Can I hide something in the app, or show something only in the app?

Yes. Some things make sense on the web but not in an app, like a banner asking people to download your app. We can hide elements like that in the app with a small CSS or JavaScript tweak. You can also build pages and offers on your website and set them to show only in the app.

Is there anything that needs special handling in the app?

A few things. Google doesn’t allow its sign-in page or Google Pay to load inside an app’s web view, so we set both up to open the way Google requires. Links that open other apps need a little extra configuration, and features that rely on the phone’s hardware are reviewed case by case. If you’re unsure about an integration, ask us, and we’ll check how it works on your site.

Does MobiLoud work with my ecommerce platform?

Yes. We build apps for stores on Shopify, Magento, WooCommerce, BigCommerce, Salesforce Commerce Cloud and dozens of other platforms, as well as headless and custom-built sites. If your website runs in a mobile browser, we can build your app on it.

How is this different from integrations in Tapcart and other app builders?

In an app builder, an integration is a connector the builder has made between its platform and another service, and it brings over part of what that service does on your site. If a service isn’t on the list, you need custom work to add it, or you leave it out of the app. With MobiLoud, the integration runs in your app exactly as it is set up on your site, inside an app our team designs for the phone.
