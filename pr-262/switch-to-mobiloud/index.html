Switch to MobiLoud

Switch to an app that keeps up with your store

Unlike app builders and custom builds, MobiLoud builds your app on your live store, no rebuilding, no second storefront to maintain. You get a custom native app with no app builder limits, and a team that looks after it for you.

Outgrown your app, or overpaying for it?

Brands usually switch to MobiLoud from two kinds of app. Some started with an app builder and ran into the limits of traditional “no-code” tools. Others paid for a custom app, built from scratch, and found that the upfront cost was only a small part of the final bill.

From an app builder

Quick to launch, but not truly in sync with your site.

  • Every change made twice, on your site and in the app
  • Apps and theme work that never made it into the app
  • Features capped by what the builder offers

From a custom app

No limits - other than the time and cost it takes to run.

  • You need developers for every change and release
  • Looked good at launch, but now lags behind
  • A second codebase that keeps costing after launch

What changes

Everything you wanted from your first app

We design and build your custom app on your existing store. You get the freedom of a custom app, with our team handling launch and upkeep.

A custom app, designed for your brand

Our team designs your tab bar, navigation, push notifications and sign-in around your store, and builds them in Swift for iOS and Kotlin for Android.

Your whole stack carries over to the app

Your extensions, plugins and customizations just work in your new app, so there’s no integration list to check and no catalog to keep in sync.

Custom features, without a custom codebase

Need biometric login, barcode scanning or a custom SDK? We scope it with you, price it before any work starts and build it into your app.

No duplicate work required

Change your store and your app follows. We handle the releases, the iOS and Android updates and the store reviews as part of the deal.

Update your app without losing your users

We’ll help you switch to a new app, with little to no disruption for your users. Your current app stays live while we build the new one. When it’s ready, we publish it as an update to your existing listings, so all your customers carry over.

What you keep, and what you leave behind

Switching to MobiLoud gives you a better way to maintain your app, and a channel that’s automatically in sync with your site, offering a better UX for your existing customers.

Can we keep our App Store listing, ratings and reviews?

You keep your App Store and Google Play listings, ratings and reviews when you switch to MobiLoud, as long as your app is in your own Apple and Google developer accounts. We publish the new app as an update to that listing, so to the stores and to your customers it’s the same app.

Do our customers need to download a new app?

Your customers don’t need to download a new app. The new version arrives as an update to the one they already have, the same way any app update does, so you keep your install base.

Will our current app stay live while you build the new one?

Your current app stays live until the new one is approved and ready to launch. We time the switch so the new version is live before you cancel your app builder or agency.

How long does it take to switch to MobiLoud?

Switching to MobiLoud takes 2 to 4 weeks from kickoff for most brands. That covers the build, your testing and sign-off, and the App Store and Google Play reviews, which usually take one to two weeks.

How much does it cost to switch?

Switching to MobiLoud starts at $799 per month, with no revenue share. Our team designs, builds, publishes and maintains your app. See our pricing page for details.

Can you rebuild the features our current app has?

Your new app keeps everything your store does, because it’s built on your store. Features that only lived in your old app, such as a barcode scanner or a custom SDK, can be built into your app on Enterprise as separate projects, which we scope and price with you before any work starts.

Which app builders can we switch from?

You can switch to MobiLoud from any app builder, including Tapcart, Shopney, Appbrew, Plobal and Superfans, or from a custom app built by an agency or your own team. MobiLoud works with Shopify and Shopify Plus, and with any other ecommerce platform or custom stack your store runs on.

Is a MobiLoud app as good as a custom native app?

A MobiLoud app is a native iOS and Android app, built in Swift and Kotlin, that loads your live store and adds native navigation, push notifications, login and other native features on top. Jack & Jones moved its custom native apps to MobiLoud and saw no drop in the app experience, with a much simpler workflow behind the scenes.

Who looks after the app once it’s live?

Our team looks after your app once it’s live. When your store changes, your app follows, and when Apple or Google release new OS versions or change their requirements, we make the native updates and ship the releases as part of your subscription.

What happens if we leave MobiLoud later?

If you leave MobiLoud later, your listings and your users stay with you. Your apps are in your own developer accounts, so a future app ships as an update to the same listing, and on request we hand back an export of your app configurations and native build settings within 30 days.

See your new app before you switch

We’ll build a live, interactive preview of your new app from your own store, so you can compare it with the app you have today.
